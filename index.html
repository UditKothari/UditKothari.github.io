Selected work

RMIT EV Living Lab · Capstone in progress

Learning-based battery dispatch

I’m building a simulation of a microgrid with solar PV, battery storage and EV charging. The study asks when the battery should charge or discharge as generation, demand and electricity costs change.

What I’m testing

The plan is to compare a learning-based controller with rule-based dispatch on peak demand, electricity cost and battery state of health. I’m also considering forecast error, communications latency and operating constraints. Supervised by Prof. Ali Morad Amani.

Research hardware · RMIT FMM Lab

Wearable biosensor hardware

Delivered a manufactured flex PCB for a sweat-sensing system designed to fit within a 12 mm eyeglass temple.

EasyEDA · LTspice

Design and testing

Estimated a 600 mV coin-cell voltage sag during BLE transmission, then changed the design to a 112 mAh LiPo with separate analog and digital LDO rails. Redesigned the flex as a T-Wing to extend the estimated diffusion time between the glucose and sodium channels to over 90 seconds. The design used TI OPA2333 amplifiers and a u-blox ANNA-B402 module. Flex PCB manufactured. Rigid version in EDA.
