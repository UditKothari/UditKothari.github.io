01 RMIT EV Living Lab · Team capstone

The battery held the load.
Charging shook the grid.

Our capstone asks how a battery should dispatch in a microgrid with solar and EV charging. In Semester 1, we built the Simulink/Simscape model and tested the electrical behaviour that any future dispatch policy would have to work with.

Microgrid control optimisation

When the islanded battery absorbed surplus solar, an 11.4 Hz oscillation grew in the voltage response. Diagnostic runs isolated the problem to the battery voltage loop under charging conditions.

Raising the proportional gain from 0.3 to 1.0 stabilised the rerun. The battery absorbed 6.38 kW and frequency settled at 50 Hz in simulation. We repeated the 60 kW discharge case to check that the change held up there as well.

My contribution. I co-led the engineering methodology, model iteration and feasibility testing. Supervisor: Prof. Ali Morad Amani.

02 RMIT FMM Lab · Research internship

Wearable biosensor hardware

I designed a mixed-signal flex PCB for an eyeglass temple, with separate glucose and sodium sensing channels. A BLE transmit burst was calculated to pull the original coin-cell rail down by 600 mV. I switched to a 112 mAh LiPo and isolated the analogue and digital supplies with separate LDO regulators.

Layout, sensing and fabrication

I separated the sensing sites with a T-wing layout, extending the estimated diffusion time between channels beyond 90 seconds. I used OPA2333 zero-drift amplifiers and a u-blox ANNA-B402 BLE module. I took the flex through mixed-signal layout in EasyEDA and fabrication and rechecked component substitutions in LTspice.

03 Siemens S7-1512C · RMIT lab

PLC-based sorting station

I wrote ladder logic for a live processing station. Limit switches, light barriers, colour sensing and conveyor feedback drove the step sequencer so each operation advanced when the plant reached the expected state.

What happens if you stop mid-move?

For a normal stop request while the robot was carrying a workpiece, the sequence let it complete delivery before halting. It required homing before restart. I integrated that behaviour alongside the gripping, processing and colour-sorting steps in TIA Portal V17.

04 Python · RMIT coursework

EV detection from smart-meter data

I built classifiers to infer EV ownership from household electricity demand profiles. I looked for charging behaviour in the shape of the load, including overnight ramps and sustained high draw.

How I compared models

I compared SVM, KNN and logistic regression on held-out households, looking at precision and recall alongside accuracy. A key test was whether the features could distinguish sustained charging patterns from other high-draw appliances.
