Problem 01

15-Second Diffusion Crisis

Fick's Laws showed H⁺ ions would cross the 5mm electrode gap in 10–15 seconds — collapsing the sodium baseline mid-measurement.

Fix → Rerouted the flex PCB into a T-Wing geometry. Diffusion time extended to >90 seconds. No chemistry changed. Geometry solved it.
Problem 02

BLE Voltage Crash

Calculated: 15mA BLE burst × 40Ω CR2032 ESR = 600mV ripple across the power plane. Op-amp outputs would saturate on every transmission.

Fix → Switched to 112mAh LiPo + Dual-LDO isolation between analog and digital domains. Solved on paper, before ordering a single part.
