P0115-Second Diffusion Crisis

Fick's Laws: H+ ions from the GOx reaction would cross the 5mm inter-electrode gap in 10 to 15 seconds. The sodium baseline would be chemically destroyed before the 60-second glucose window even closed. Chemistry does not care about your schedule.

SolvedRerouted the flex PCB into a T-Wing geometry. Physical isolation stretched diffusion time to >90 seconds. Geometry fixed what chemistry couldn't.
P02BLE Voltage Crash

Calculated: 15mA BLE burst × 40Ω CR2032 ESR = 600mV sag per transmission. Op-amp outputs would rail on every BLE packet. The sensor would broadcast garbage precisely when it was being read.

SolvedSwapped CR2032 for 112mAh LiPo and Dual-LDO analog/digital isolation. Figured it out before a single component was ordered.

FMM Research Lab

RMIT Functional Materials & Microsystems · 2025 to Present

The actual problem

Twice during the project a component we'd designed around went out of stock right when we hit the ordering stage. Each time: find an equivalent, validate against the schematic, re-run the sims. It's uncomfortable. It also builds hardware instinct faster than any textbook.

What I built

Full schematic to PCB flow in EasyEDA across multiple revisions. Analog front-end with TI OPA2333 zero-drift op-amps, dual-LDO power isolation, BLE radio via u-blox ANNA-B402, and a flex PCB geometry that solved a diffusion physics problem by rerouting copper.

What I took from it

Mixed-signal layout discipline. How to protect analog circuits from digital switching noise before fabrication, not after. How to read electrochemistry literature and turn it into hardware constraints. Supply chain is a real engineering variable.
