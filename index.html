01 Featured Project

Multiplexed Wearable
Conductometric Sensor

RMIT FMM Lab · 2025–Present Supervised by Prof. Madhu Bhaskaran

Diabetes management still relies on finger-pricks. CGMs require needles under skin. Athletes have no real-time electrolyte data. Sweat changes all of that — it carries glucose and sodium without a single drop of blood.

The challenge: fit the entire sensing system inside an eyeglass temple. That's a <12mm width constraint. Every standard approach failed on paper before we ordered a single component.

The Sensor Physics
Na⁺
Sodium Detection
Direct conductometry. Na⁺/Cl⁻ ions act as charge carriers — immediate, steady-state resistance drop across 40µm gold interdigitated electrodes.
C₆H₁₂O₆
Glucose Detection
Enzymatic conductometry via Glucose Oxidase (GOx). Generates highly conductive H⁺ ions. Requires a strict 60-second transient measurement window.
Why Conductometry?

Standard amperometric sensors need 3 electrodes and a bulky potentiostat. They mathematically cannot fit our footprint.

Conductometry condenses the entire Analog Front End (AFE) to a single transimpedance amplifier — 2 electrodes, minimal circuitry, fits the temple.

Final Architecture
MCU u-blox ANNA-B402 (nRF5 SDK)
AFE TI OPA2333 Zero-Drift Op-Amps
Power 112mAh LiPo · Dual-LDO isolation
Wireless BLE (2.4GHz)
Electrodes 40µm gold interdigitated array
Status Flex PCB manufactured · Rigid board in EDA
02 How I Think

Two failures predicted.
Zero prototypes wasted.

Before ordering a single component, I ran the physics. The math revealed two project-killing problems. Here's how I solved them on paper.

Problem 01

The 15-Second Diffusion Crisis

What Fick's Laws Said

H⁺ ions from the glucose reaction would migrate across a 5mm gap in just 10–15 seconds — destroying the sodium baseline long before the required 60-second glucose measurement window ended.

The Fix

Rerouted the flexible PCB into a "T-Wing" topology — physically isolating the 40µm electrode arrays. Result: theoretical diffusion travel time extended to >90 seconds. Clean, isolated 60-second window secured.

Geometry solves chemistry better than digital filtering.
Problem 02

The Bluetooth Voltage Crash

The Calculated Failure
Vdrop = IBLE × ESRCR2032 = 15mA × 40Ω = 600mV sag

Every BLE transmission would ripple 600mV through the power plane, spiking the op-amp outputs and masking the physiological signal entirely.

The Fix

Replaced CR2032 with a 112mAh LiPo (near-zero ESR) and added Dual-LDO isolation — mathematically separating the analog and digital power domains before a single board was fabbed.

Protect analog circuits from hungry digital radios. Always.
