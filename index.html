P0115-Second Diffusion Crisis

Fick's Laws: H⁺ ions from the GOx reaction would cross the 5mm inter-electrode gap in 10–15 seconds. The sodium baseline would be chemically destroyed before the 60-second glucose window closed.

SolvedRerouted the flex PCB into a T-Wing geometry. Physical isolation extended diffusion time to >90 seconds. Geometry solved chemistry.
P02BLE Voltage Crash

Calculated: 15mA BLE burst × 40Ω CR2032 ESR = 600mV sag per transmission. Op-amp outputs would rail and mask every physiological reading.

SolvedReplaced CR2032 with 112mAh LiPo + Dual-LDO analog/digital isolation. All on paper, before a single component was ordered.

RMIT Functional Materials & Microsystems · 2025–Present

+

Designing and building a wearable multi-analyte biosensor under Prof. Bhaskaran and Prof. Sriram — from EDA schematic through to flex PCB manufacture. Mathematical modelling before every fabrication decision.

Mixed-Signal PCBEasyEDABLELTspice
Real challenge

Twice during the project, a component we'd designed around went out of stock by the time we reached the ordering stage. Each time, it forced a rapid design pivot — finding an equivalent part, validating it against the schematic, and re-running simulations. Uncomfortable, but it built genuine speed and hardware instinct fast.

What I built

Full schematic-to-PCB flow in EasyEDA across multiple revisions. Analog front-end with TI OPA2333 zero-drift op-amps, dual-LDO power isolation, BLE radio integration via u-blox ANNA-B402, and a flex PCB topology that solved a diffusion physics problem geometrically.

What I learned

Mixed-signal layout discipline. How to protect sensitive analog circuits from digital switching noise mathematically, before fabrication. How to read electrochemistry literature and translate it into hardware constraints. And that supply chain is a real engineering variable.
